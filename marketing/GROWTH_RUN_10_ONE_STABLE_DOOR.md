# SOVRAIL Growth Run 10 — One Stable Door

## Positioning

**Your app should not need a new integration every time the provider changes.**

SOVRAIL is the Wilkerson Collective-controlled service boundary between WCL assets and local or third-party APIs. The consuming product knows one stable interface and a scoped SOVRAIL credential; provider secrets remain server-side.

## 30-second enterprise video concept

**0–4s — Fragmentation**
Multiple WCL product tiles each show separate provider wires and credentials.
Overlay: `TOO MANY PROVIDERS. TOO MANY SECRETS.`

**4–10s — Convergence**
All product traffic converges into one SOVRAIL gateway.
VO: “Give your software one stable service boundary.”

**10–21s — Control plane**
Show local-first routing, optional paid-provider fallback, exact-result cache, quotas/budgets, circuit breakers, idempotency, and audit records.
VO: “Route approved requests locally when available, fall back when configured, control budgets, prevent duplicate paid work, and keep an audit trail.”

**21–27s — Provider abstraction**
Swap provider icons behind the gateway while the product-side interface stays unchanged.
Overlay: `ONE INTERFACE. CONTROLLED ROUTING.`

**27–30s — End card**
`SOVRAIL — Sovereign API Runtime & Integration Layer`
CTA: `Build against one stable interface.`

## Claims guardrail

Do not imply SOVRAIL bypasses third-party authorization, billing, rate limits, or terms. Any request actually executed by a commercial upstream remains subject to that provider’s requirements and pricing. Local execution, caching, idempotency, budget controls, and provider selection can reduce unnecessary upstream usage but do not make paid APIs free.
